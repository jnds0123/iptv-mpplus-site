Orpheus Property Console

Working prototype. The guest television and the operations console run off one shared guest record — act on one, watch the other change.

Try this: order dinner on the television, or complete Pre-arrival on the guest’s phone. Then open the Hotel console: the kitchen order, the service ticket, the folio line and the signed documents are already there, because one record sits behind every screen.

Remote

Your keyboard arrows, Enter and Backspace drive the screen too — the same four inputs a hotel remote has.

What this is

The guest's own phone, opened from a link in the confirmation. A web page, not an app — nobody installs software for a two-night stay, and it has to work on iOS.

The scan is parsed on the device. What reaches the platform is structured fields, not a photograph to be read again later.

Finish it and the front desk sees Room 1502 flip from Not started to Scanned & signed, with both documents in the vault on a retention clock.
