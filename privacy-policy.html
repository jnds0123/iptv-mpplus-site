Privacy Policy — IPTV Multimedia Player Plus

Effective date: July 15, 2026

IPTV Multimedia Player Plus ("IPTV MP+", "the app") is a media player for Android phones, tablets, and Android TV / Google TV devices. It plays content from playlists and services that you supply. The app ships with no content, no accounts, and no bundled providers.

Data we collect

None. The app has no analytics, no advertising SDKs, no crash-reporting service, and no developer-operated servers. We never see who you are or what you watch.

Data stored on your device

To work offline-first, the app stores the following only on your device:

  • Playlist sources you add (M3U/M3U8 URLs, Xtream Codes server address, username and password, optional XMLTV EPG URLs).
  • A local cache of the channel, movie, and series listings from those sources.
  • Your profiles, favorites, resume positions, and app settings.

This data never leaves your device except as described below, and is removed when you clear the app's storage or uninstall it.

Network connections the app makes

  • Your configured providers: the app connects directly to the playlist, Xtream, EPG, and stream URLs you enter, solely to fetch listings and play media. Your credentials are sent only to the server you configured them for.
  • Chromecast: if you cast, playback is handed to your Cast device via Google Play services on your local network. Google's own privacy policy applies to Google Play services.

Third parties

We do not sell, share, or transmit any personal data to third parties. The app contains no third-party advertising or tracking.

Children

The app is a general-audience media player and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Your content

You are responsible for the playlists and subscriptions you add. Use only content you are legally entitled to access.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new effective date.
